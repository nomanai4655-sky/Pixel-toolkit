Before you submit an official photo

Many governments (Canada, for example) do not allow retouching, filters, or background replacement in official passport or visa photos. For a real application, keep Enhance, Beauty, and Replace-background off — just set the correct size and a plain background color, and confirm your country's current rules before submitting.

When printing, ask for the photo at its actual size (no "fit to page" scaling).
